Rain Jacket Care

A rain jacket that stops beading water is not broken, it just needs a little care. Clean it, refresh the water-repellent finish and fix small problems early.

02.1

Why jackets wet out

The outer fabric has a durable water repellent (DWR) finish that makes rain bead up. Dirt, sweat and wear wear it down. Once the face fabric soaks up water, the jacket feels clammy and breathes poorly, even though the membrane is still waterproof.

02.2

Wash it

  • Close all zips and Velcro, and empty pockets.
  • Use a technical wash or a small amount of mild liquid soap, never fabric softener.
  • Wash on a gentle cycle at the temperature on the care label and rinse twice.
02.3

Revive the finish

After washing, many finishes come back with gentle heat: tumble dry on low or press with a cool iron through a towel, if the care label allows. If water still soaks in, apply a spray-on or wash-in re-proofer and follow the instructions.

02.4

Small repairs

Tiny tears can be sealed with repair tape made for waterproof fabric. Peeling seam tape can be re-glued with seam sealer. A zip that sticks often just needs cleaning and a little zip lubricant.

02.5

Store it well

Hang your jacket somewhere dry and airy rather than leaving it crumpled in a bag. Make sure it is completely dry before storing so the lining does not get musty. Packed jackets should be unpacked when you get home.
